Features & Controls
  • Click any state/province: Smoothly zooms into the regional view.
  • Toggle Layer Pills: Switch between Counties/Divisions, Cities, Districts/Ridings, and Zip Codes/FSAs.
  • Live Search Bar: Try typing 90210, Miami, Toronto, or M5V!
  • Pan & Zoom: Drag with mouse, scroll wheel or pinch to zoom into any detail.
  • Stitched Cities: View true composite boundary outlines formed from member postal areas.
  • Export: Instant standalone SVG export for any view.
